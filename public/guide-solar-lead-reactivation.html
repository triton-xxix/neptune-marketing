Why Lead Reactivation Matters for Solar Companies

Solar installation companies spend thousands on lead generation every month. But what happens to the leads that don't convert immediately? Most solar businesses have databases filled with dormant leads—homeowners who showed interest but never booked an installation. These leads aren't dead; they just need the right reactivation strategy.

Our 10-point lead reactivation checklist has helped solar companies recover millions in potential revenue by systematically re-engaging dormant leads and converting them into paying customers.

The 10-Point Lead Reactivation Framework

1

Audit Your Lead Database

Start by identifying all leads that haven't been contacted in 30+ days. Segment them by interest level, property type, and previous objections. Solar leads often have specific characteristics—roof condition, energy bills, and installation timeline—that should be tracked for effective reactivation.

2

Clean and Update Contact Information

Verify email addresses, phone numbers, and property details. Solar leads may have moved or changed contact details. Use email verification tools and phone number validation to ensure your messages reach the right people.

3

Segment by Objection Type

Group leads based on why they didn't convert initially. Common solar objections include price concerns, roof suitability, waiting for better technology, or timing issues. Each segment needs a tailored reactivation approach.

4

Create Compelling Reactivation Offers

Develop time-sensitive offers that address common solar buyer concerns. This could include free roof assessments, updated energy savings calculations, information about new government incentives, or flexible financing options.

5

Craft Personalised Email Campaigns

Send personalised emails that acknowledge their previous interest and provide new value. Include updated energy savings projections, recent installation case studies from similar properties, or news about rising energy costs that make solar more attractive.

6

Implement SMS Follow-ups

Use SMS for quick, attention-grabbing messages. Text messages have a 98% open rate and are perfect for short, urgent offers like "Energy prices are rising. Book your free solar assessment this week and save £500."

7

Leverage Retargeting Ads

Run targeted social media and Google ads to stay visible to dormant leads. Show them testimonials, new installation photos, and updated offers. Retargeting keeps your solar company top-of-mind when they're ready to reconsider.

8

Make Personal Phone Calls

Nothing beats a personal touch. Have your sales team call dormant leads with a specific reason—new financing options, updated government incentives, or seasonal offers. Reference their previous interest to show you remember them.

9

Provide Updated Energy Savings Calculations

Energy costs have changed significantly. Provide updated savings projections based on current electricity rates. Show homeowners exactly how much they could save by switching to solar now versus waiting longer.

10

Track, Measure, and Optimise

Monitor your reactivation campaign metrics—open rates, response rates, appointments booked, and conversions. Use A/B testing to refine your messaging and offers. Continuously improve based on what works best for your solar business.
