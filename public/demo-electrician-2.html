Frequently Asked Questions

Are you NICEIC registered?

Yes, we are fully NICEIC registered and certified. All work comes with compliance certificates and meets BS 7671 standards.

Yes, we provide 24/7 emergency electrical services for dangerous faults, power outages, and urgent repairs.

Absolutely. We provide Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICRs) for landlords, homeowners, and businesses with full documentation.
