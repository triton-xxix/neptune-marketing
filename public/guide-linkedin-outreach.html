The LinkedIn Outreach System for Trades Businesses

How to book 2-3 coffee dates per week with construction, roofing, and trades business owners using a simple 4-message sequence.

📅 March 2026 ⏱️ 15 min read 🎯 Lead Generation

The Overview

This is the exact system we use at Neptune Marketing to connect with trades business owners (roofers, electricians, builders, landscapers) and book qualified sales calls. It's designed to be manual first, delegatable second — so you can run it yourself to learn what works, then hand it to a VA once proven.

The system uses LinkedIn Sales Navigator to find prospects, a 4-message sequence to build trust, and a performance-based offer to close the conversation. No automation tools. No risk of LinkedIn bans. Just consistent daily action.

Target Criteria

Before sending a single connection request, get crystal clear on who you're targeting. These filters will save you hours of wasted outreach.

Sales Navigator Filters

Filter Category Setting
Company Headcount 11-50 employees
Geography United Kingdom
Industry Construction (covers roofers, electricians, builders, landscapers)
Job Titles Owner, CEO, Founder, Director, Managing Director
Seniority Owner, CXO, Director, Partner
Posted on LinkedIn Past 30 days (proves they're active)
Exclude Marketing, Agency, Consultant, Recruiter, HR

⚠️ Why These Filters Matter

11-50 employees is the sweet spot — big enough to have dormant leads worth reactivating, small enough that the owner still makes decisions. Companies with 1-10 employees often don't have enough lead volume. Companies with 50+ employees move too slowly.

The 4-Message Sequence

This is the core of the system. Each message has a specific job. Don't deviate until you've sent 100+ sequences and know what works.

💬 Message 1 — Connection Request

Hi [Name], noticed you're in the [industry] space. Would love to connect and hear your take on where the industry's heading in 2026.
⏱️ Send immediately after finding them. Wait 4 hours to 1 day before Message 2.

Option A (Recommended): Send with no message. Higher acceptance rate, less "salesy."

Option B: Use the template above if they post frequently and seem engaged.

💬 Message 2 — Lead Magnet Delivery

Thanks for connecting [Name]. I put together a quick case study showing how a B2B services company recovered £200K from 18-month-old leads using AI + SMS — most construction/trades businesses are sitting on £10K-£100K in "dead" leads without realizing it. Thought you might find it useful: https://neptunemarketing.ai/case-study-200k.html Would love your thoughts if any of it resonates with your situation.
⏱️ Send 1 day after they accept. Wait 3-4 days before Message 3.

💬 Message 3 — Follow-Up

Hey [Name], did you get a chance to check out the lead reactivation case study? Curious if the numbers resonated — most business owners I speak with are surprised how much revenue is sitting in their old quote requests and inquiries.
⏱️ Send 3-4 days after Message 2 (if no response). Wait 3-4 days before Message 4.

💬 Message 4 — Coffee Date Ask

[Name], I've been helping a few [industry] companies turn their old leads into new sales using AI and SMS. The model is performance-based — you only pay if we actually generate revenue from your dormant leads. Would love to jump on a quick 15-min call to see if I can help you too. How's [day] looking this week?
⏱️ Send 3-4 days after Message 3. This is your ask — stop here if no response.

The Daily Checklist

Consistency beats intensity. Do this every weekday for 30 minutes.

☀️ Morning Routine (30 minutes total)

Open Sales Navigator saved search "Trades — Database Reactivation"
Send 5 connection requests (no more than 5 per day)
Check tracker for connections that accepted — send Message 2 if it's been 1 day
Check tracker for Message 2 recipients — send Message 3 if it's been 3-4 days and no response
Check tracker for Message 3 recipients — send Message 4 if it's been 3-4 days and no response
Spend 10 minutes engaging: Like/comment on 3-5 posts from target industry
Update tracker after every action (critical)

The Rules

Break these and you'll get flagged by LinkedIn or burn your pipeline.

❌ Don't ✅ Do Instead
Send more than 5 connections/day Stick to 5/day to stay under LinkedIn radar
Send Message 2 immediately after connecting Wait 1 day minimum
Send more than 4 messages without response Stop at 4, move on
Copy-paste exact same message Slightly personalize each one
Use words like "buy", "purchase", "deal" early Lead with value, not sales pitch
Message people who haven't posted in 30+ days Filter to "Past 30 days" only

Troubleshooting

"I'm not getting connections accepted"

  • Check your profile photo is professional
  • Make sure your headline is optimized (not just "Owner at X")
  • Try Option A (no message) for connection requests
  • Ensure you're targeting people who posted in last 30 days

"People accept but don't respond"

  • This is normal — expect 10-20% response rate
  • Make sure Message 2 provides clear value (case study link)
  • Follow up consistently (Msg 3 and 4)
  • Not everyone will respond — that's okay, focus on volume

"LinkedIn is limiting my connection requests"

  • Stop for 24 hours immediately
  • Reduce to 3-4/day temporarily
  • Make sure you're not copy-pasting identical messages
  • Engage with posts more (builds trust with algorithm)
