The 10-Point Lead Reactivation Checklist

1

Audit Your Dormant Lead Database

Export all leads from the last 12-24 months who didn't convert. Segment by lead source (website, referrals, PPC, etc.) and original inquiry type (repair, replacement, inspection). A clean, organised database is the foundation of successful reactivation.

2

Verify Contact Information

Run your list through a verification service to remove invalid numbers and emails. For roofing companies, 15-20% of contact data degrades every year. Cleaning your list before outreach protects your sender reputation and saves money.

3

Segment by Lead Temperature

Not all dormant leads are equal. Segment by:
• Hot: Requested quote but never scheduled (0-3 months old)
• Warm: Expressed interest but went silent (3-12 months old)
• Cold: Old inquiries, referrals that never responded (12+ months old)

4

Craft Your Reactivation Message

Your first message should be personal, brief, and value-focused. No hard selling. Example: "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company]. You requested a roofing quote a few months back. We're now offering free drone inspections — would you like to book yours?"

5

Set Up Multi-Channel Follow-Up

Don't rely on just one channel. The most effective sequence:
• Day 1: SMS
• Day 3: Email (if you have it)
• Day 5: Second SMS with different angle
• Day 7: Voice call or voicemail drop

6

Use AI for Speed and Scale

Manual follow-up is too slow. Use AI SMS tools to send personalised messages within seconds of a response. AI can handle initial qualification, answer common questions, and book appointments directly into your calendar — 24/7.

7

Create Urgency Without Pressure

Roofing is seasonal. Use real urgency drivers: "We're booking 3 weeks ahead due to storm damage repairs" or "Our March inspection slots are 80% full." Never use fake scarcity — roofing customers can smell it.

8

Track and Measure Everything

Monitor these KPIs weekly:
• Delivery rate (should be >95%)
• Response rate (target: 20-30%)
• Appointment booking rate (target: 10-15%)
• Conversion to sale (target: 25-40%)
• Revenue per reactivated lead

9

Have a "Not Now" Nurture Sequence

80% of respondents will say "not now" rather than "no." Don't delete them. Move them to a long-term nurture sequence: quarterly check-ins, seasonal maintenance tips, storm damage alerts. Many will convert 6-12 months later.

10

Systematise for Continuous Reactivation

Lead reactivation shouldn't be a one-time campaign. Build it into your weekly operations:
• Every Monday: Run last week's non-converters through reactivation
• Every month: Run a batch campaign on 90-day+ old leads
• Every quarter: Full database reactivation sweep

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Blasting everyone at once: This triggers spam filters and burns your list. Pace your sends (50-100/day max).
  • Generic copy-paste messages: Personalise with first name, specific service they inquired about, and local area reference.
  • Giving up after one message: It takes 3-5 touchpoints on average. Most roofing companies quit after one.
  • Not following up fast enough: When someone responds, reply within 5 minutes. Response rates drop 80% after 1 hour.
